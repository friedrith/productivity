# Raycast

## Extensions

- [Arc Browser](https://www.raycast.com/the-browser-company/arc): to go to a specific tab and even make search onlines
- [Dub](https://www.raycast.com/dubinc/dub): to minify urls
- [Github](https://www.raycast.com/raycast/github): to open specific repositories
- [Color picker](https://www.raycast.com/thomas/color-picker): to pick a color
- [Paste as](https://github.com/raycast/extensions/tree/40c3b7048ce7bfebbeffcadb1d5a276ff1b865c5/extensions/paste-as-plain-text/)
- [Spotify](https://www.raycast.com/mattisssa/spotify-player): to control Spotify
- [ChatGPT](https://www.raycast.com/abielzulio/chatgpt)
- [Apple reminder](https://www.raycast.com/raycast/apple-reminders)
- [Open folder](https://www.raycast.com/timothy_boye/open-folders): hard to have shortcuts for specific folders on macOS
- [Password Generator](https://www.raycast.com/joshuaiz/password-generator)
